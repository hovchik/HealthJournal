# Medical Disclaimer

HealthJournal is a **personal journal**, not a medical device.

- Nothing in this app is **medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment**.
- The app does **not** replace a doctor, nurse, pharmacist, or any other
  qualified healthcare professional. Always ask one before acting on
  anything you see here.
- AI-generated summaries and trend hints are **probabilistic**. They can be
  wrong, incomplete, or misleading. Treat them as discussion notes for your
  doctor — never as a conclusion.
- Do **not** start, stop, or change any medication or dose based on the app.
- The app does **not** monitor you in real time and will not alert emergency
  services. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (e.g.
  112 / 911 / 102) or go to the nearest emergency department.
- Entries you share with third-party AI providers (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini,
  DeepSeek) leave your device and are subject to their terms. Disable Cloud
  AI in Settings if you don't want this.

By using this app you confirm you understand these limits.
